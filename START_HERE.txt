Quorum — meeting minutes on this computer

Unzip this archive. Open the one folder you get.
Double-click Start Quorum (Start Quorum.command on a Mac, Start Quorum.bat on Windows, or start-quorum.sh on Linux).
The meeting console opens at http://127.0.0.1:4840.

Complete Setup once (organization, your name, roster, how long to keep recordings).
Tap Dry-run to practice a whole meeting.
After the meeting: Email, Download, or Print the minutes.

Nothing leaves this computer unless you tap Polish with SpaceXAI.

If Start Quorum does not run, open a terminal in this folder and type: python3 -m quorum
Python 3.11 or newer is required.
